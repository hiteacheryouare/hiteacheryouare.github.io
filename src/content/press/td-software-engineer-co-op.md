Ryan Mullin will join TD as a software engineer co-op on the bank's Global Technology & Solutions team. He will work from TD's Mount Laurel, N.J., offices from January through June 2027.

The six-month placement is Mullin's first co-op at Northeastern University, where he studies computer science and business administration. Northeastern's co-op program builds full-time work terms into the undergraduate degree, and it was one of the reasons Mullin chose the university.
