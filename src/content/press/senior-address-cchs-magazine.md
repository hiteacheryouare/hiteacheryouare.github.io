An excerpt from the senior address Ryan Mullin delivered at Camden Catholic High School's commencement in June 2025 appears in the 2025 issue of CCHS Magazine, in a feature on the graduating class titled ["Graduating Students with Purpose: The Camden Catholic Class of 2025."](https://issuu.com/camdencatholic/docs/graduating_students_with_purpose_the_camden_catho) The feature opens on page 10 and carries the excerpt on page 14. It was written by Kate Madden Bieg '98, who was one of Mullin's mentors at the school.

Mullin's address is titled "To Be Better Tomorrow." The full version is [on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqXdyTZGBvk).

At Camden Catholic, Mullin worked in the school's marketing and media operations for two years, then became its admissions project manager in June 2024 and held the role through graduation.
